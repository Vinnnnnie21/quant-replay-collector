# Stop v1.6 at testable strategy hypotheses

QRC v1.6 produces reproducible quantitative behavior profiles and, when its evidence gates are met, testable strategy hypotheses. It does not produce or claim a tradable quantitative strategy. A Setup-version hypothesis may summarize its three-timeframe context, stable entry and exit behavior indicators, research thresholds, applicability range, matched outcome evidence, maturity, limitations, and the next evidence required.

The only v1.6 maturity labels for this output are `仅行为画像`, `探索性策略假设`, and `待前瞻验证`. Behavior-model probabilities remain descriptions of the user's historical choices, and event-study differences remain diagnostic outcome evidence. Neither is renamed as a buy, sell, or profit signal.

Creating a tradable strategy requires a later validation stage that joins entry and exit rules with execution costs, position sizing, risk constraints, genuinely untouched forward data, portfolio interaction, and failure monitoring. Deferring that stage limits the apparent ambition of v1.6, but prevents an explanatory research model from being mistaken for an investable system.
