# Use the next decision-timeframe bar open for entry counterfactuals

Entry-event research evaluates both ENTRY and REJECT judgments from the open of the next bar on the Setup's decision timeframe. All outcome paths are direction-adjusted so favorable movement is positive for both long and short research. Actual replay fills and realized trade performance remain separate outputs and never replace the standardized counterfactual price. Samples without a next bar have unavailable outcomes. This sacrifices exact reproduction of an acted trade, but creates a common executable comparison for decisions that did and did not produce an order.
