# Use the next decision-timeframe bar open for exit counterfactuals

Exit-event research evaluates both EXIT_NOW and HOLD judgments from the open of the next decision-timeframe bar, then observes direction-adjusted continuation over the same fixed 1-, 3-, 5-, 10-, and 20-bar horizons used by entry research. Positive continuation means holding would have remained favorable; negative continuation means exiting would have avoided adverse movement. Post-decision MFE represents missed favorable space and MAE represents avoided adverse space, with their first occurrence recorded. Actual exit fills, realized performance, and costed strategy results remain separate.
